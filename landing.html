Why it's different

Built so that nothing exists to leak.

Most messengers protect the message and keep the map. GhostLink removes the map entirely.

No Servers

Messages travel straight between devices over a direct encrypted link. There is no inbox in the middle to subpoena, breach, or shut down — because there is no middle.

Direct P2P mesh

End-to-End Encrypted

AES-256-GCM over an ECDH P-256 key exchange, with keys generated and held on your device. Ciphertext is authenticated — tampering is detected, not quietly decrypted.

AES-256-GCM · ECDH P-256

No Metadata

No account, no phone number, no email. Nothing records who spoke to whom or when, because nothing is in a position to watch. Your identity is a key you generate — not a profile we keep.

How it works

Three steps. Thirty seconds. No signup.

Generate your key

GhostLink creates a private key on your device the first time it runs. It never leaves. There is no account because your key is your identity.

Share your link

Exchange a short invite code or QR with the person you want to talk to — in person, over any channel. The key exchange (ECDH P-256) happens between your devices alone.

Talk, vanish, repeat

Every message is AES-256-GCM encrypted and authenticated, then sent peer-to-peer. Close the app and the conversation exists only where you both chose to keep it.
